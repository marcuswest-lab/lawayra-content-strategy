A new chapter, opening to the family first

We've been quietly building something for a long time.

For years, LaWayra has been a place people came to, and then went home from. Now we're opening up a different way to belong — a community, a way of life, and for a small group, a long-term home on the land.

You already know what happens when the retreat ends. Daily life rushes back in. Old patterns wait at the door. The insights start to feel further away.

This is for the people who've already been here. Who felt something real. And who want a way to stay close to it — whether that's from your living room, a season with us in Colombia, or something more permanent.

What we've learned after 2,500+ guests

The ceremony opens the door. Integration is what carries you through it. A retreat alone, no matter how powerful, isn't enough to make the change last.

What the medicine reveals

  • Self-awareness and a clearer sense of direction
  • Release of emotions long held below the surface
  • A shift in how you see habits, relationships, and identity
  • A softer relationship with anxiety, low moods, and old coping patterns
  • A nervous system that finally feels at home in the body

What happens without integration

  • The initial clarity fades as daily life takes over
  • Old habits and coping patterns quietly return
  • Insights stay intellectual instead of becoming embodied
  • Emotional processing remains incomplete or unresolved
  • The experience becomes a memory instead of a turning point

integration coaches, when you need deeper support

Sometimes the path asks for one-on-one guidance. Our family has access to a hand-picked circle of coaches and therapists — each one trained in this work, each one trusted by Sam and our team.

Monica

Head of Facilitation
Masters in Psychology & Neuroscience

Monica brings a rare combination of clinical depth and direct ceremonial experience. Her work weaves psychodynamic and schema-informed therapy with inner child work, somatic practices, and mindfulness — supporting identity shifts, relational patterns, and the move from insight into embodied change.

Ashley

Licensed Clinical Social Worker
Musical Therapist

Ashley has over fifteen years of experience working with complex trauma. Her trauma-trained, non-diagnostic approach draws on Parts Work, psychodynamic music therapy, and somatic release — with particular care for survivors of abuse, foster care, and those healing from cycles of addiction.

Valerio

Integration Counselor
Somatic Experiencing & IFS

Valerio's work is grounded in Internal Family Systems and Somatic Experiencing — honoring the wisdom of the body and the natural intelligence of the psyche. He focuses on relationships, attachment, nervous system regulation, and the shadow work that often follows transformative experience.

questions guests usually ask

Is this only for people who've already been to LaWayra?

For now, yes. We're opening to the family first — guests who've already sat with us — before anyone else. If you haven't been to a retreat yet but want to, reach out and we'll point you to the right place.

How much time does the Circle take each week?

As much or as little as you need. Some members come to every call. Others drop in when life allows. The container is here — you set the rhythm.

What's the difference between Circle, Coliving, and Founding Member?

Circle is for staying connected from home. Coliving is for a season with us on the land. Founding Member is for those who want to secure a long-term place at LaWayra and help us build what's next. Our team will walk you through each on the call.

What's the pricing?

We share pricing on the call so we can match the right option to what you're looking for. Founding family gets the best access on every tier — the team will walk you through them.

Who runs the calls and coaching?

Sam, Monica, and our wider team of facilitators and coaches. Everyone who holds space here has done the work themselves.
