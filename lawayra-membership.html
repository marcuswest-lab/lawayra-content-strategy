A new chapter, opening to our community first

We've been quietly building something for a long time.

For years, LaWayra has been a place people came to for a reset — and then went home from. Now we're opening a different way to stay connected — an online community, a set of practices, and ongoing education to help you keep growing long after the reset ends.

You already know how it goes. Daily life rushes back in. Old patterns wait at the door. The clarity you found starts to feel further away.

This is for the people who've already started this work. Who felt something real. And who want a way to stay close to it — with an online community, guides, and tools that keep the momentum going, wherever you are.

What we've learned helping 2,500+ people grow

A breakthrough opens the door. What you do next is what carries you through it. A single reset, no matter how powerful, isn't enough to make change last on its own.

What real growth looks like

  • Self-awareness and a clearer sense of direction
  • Letting go of patterns that quietly hold you back
  • A shift in how you see habits, relationships, and identity
  • A kinder relationship with stress and old coping patterns
  • Feeling more grounded and at home in yourself

What happens without support

  • The initial motivation fades as daily life takes over
  • Old habits and coping patterns quietly return
  • Insights stay intellectual instead of becoming action
  • Progress stalls without community and accountability
  • The breakthrough becomes a memory instead of a turning point

guides, for when you need deeper support

Sometimes growth asks for more personal guidance. Members have access to a hand-picked circle of guides and facilitators — each one experienced in this work, each one trusted by Sam and our team.

Monica

Head of Guidance
Masters in Psychology & Neuroscience

Monica brings a rare combination of depth and lived experience. Her work weaves reflective and schema-informed practices with inner-child work, somatic practices, and mindfulness — supporting identity shifts, healthier relationships, and the move from insight into embodied change.

Ashley

Facilitator
Sound & Somatic Practitioner

Ashley has over fifteen years of experience supporting people through major life change. Her grounded, non-diagnostic approach draws on Parts Work, sound-based practices, and somatic release — with particular care for those navigating difficult chapters and building healthier patterns.

Valerio

Somatic Counselor
Somatic Experiencing & IFS

Valerio's work is grounded in Internal Family Systems and Somatic Experiencing — honoring the wisdom of the body and the natural intelligence of the psyche. He focuses on relationships, attachment, nervous system regulation, and the shadow work that often follows big life change.

questions members usually ask

Is this only for people already in the LaWayra community?

For now, yes. We're opening to our community first before anyone else. If you're new and want to join, reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.

How much time does membership take each week?

As much or as little as you need. Some members come to every call. Others drop in when life allows. The community is here — you set the rhythm.

Where does the community meet?

Everything happens online. The live community calls, group sessions, workshops, and the member series are all virtual, so you can take part from anywhere in the world — all you need is an internet connection.

What's the difference between the three memberships?

Community is the easiest way in — the private community, the self-guided series, and the resource library. Circle adds live weekly support: community calls, group sessions, and workshops. Circle + Coaching adds one-on-one time with our guides. You can move up or down anytime.

How does billing work? Can I cancel?

Every membership is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime — no long-term contract. Founding members keep their pricing locked in for as long as they stay.

Who runs the calls and sessions?

Sam, Monica, and our wider team of guides. Everyone who holds space here has done the work themselves.

Do you offer in-person experiences too?

Yes. Members get one complimentary in-person community gathering each year, and we host in-person experiences at our center in Colombia throughout the year. If you'd like to join us in person, reach out to the team and we'll help you find the right dates.
